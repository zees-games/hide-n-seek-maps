# Import notes

How each imported map was made, with the importer flags used and the bot results. Maintainer notes; the README has the credits table.

`tools/import_bsp.py` converts a Source 1 `.bsp` into a document here (brushes become boxes, props become catalogue props, `prop_door_rotating` becomes a real door, player starts become seeker and hider spawns; art is replaced with the game's CC0 Kenney props). Imported documents are not written by `build_maps.py`; rerun the importer with the original `.bsp`. Flags: `--style`, `--slide-doors`, `--scale=k`, `--drop-door=x,z`, `--cover=N`, `--drop-kind=<id>`, `--thin=<id>:N`.

`hs_mountainresort` (gm_mountainresort, "Mountain Resort", by prvec, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2312309380) was converted with `import_bsp.py gm_mountainresort.bsp hs_mountainresort "Mountain Resort" prvec https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2312309380 --terrain --floor=1.2 --min-size=0.5 --cover=20`: the seekers' floor has room for two pieces of cover, so the rest stand on the hillside they walk to, and the 28 author starts on ground the seekers cannot walk to are replaced by starts on walked ground. Two seekers on importer pads in a terrain hole 3 m under the deck and three hiders in a cut-off room were then moved onto the deck by dot-map-convert's `tools/fit_doc.py tools maps/hs_mountainresort.json --join-spawns` (the BSP is not on disk; the committed document was fixed in place). Workshop terms on the page: "Do not reupload any content. Also it cannot be used in paid work you do without my consent"; no licence permitting redistribution is stated. **Flagged: the owner decided to convert it anyway; the arrangement is credit, link and removal on request, and the map is removed on request by the author.** Only the layout is used (boxes, doors, starts); every texture, model and sound is a free replacement or absent.

`hs_apartment` (ph_apartment by Tib, https://gamebanana.com/mods/61479) is Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs 4.0; credit, a link and removal on request is the arrangement, **this map is removed on request by the author**. Every texture and model is replaced by the game's own CC0 stand-ins. It is in the pack but **not banded in `headless_bots`** (`HS_BOTS_MAPS`): a flat 16 by 15 m is small for the bands. Its best setting (`--cover=40 --cover-gap=1.5 --cover-clear=1.0 --cover-seed=11`, which the document is built with) gives hiders a mean of 112 s and 11 of 24 won, 26% never found, but 89 of 140 hiders walk the 8 m (band 112), somebody is caught in the first 3 s of a seek in some rounds, and a stand-in lingers 11.8 s beside a door panel (band 9). Moving the seekers or hiders (`--seekers-at`, `--hiders-at`, `--hiders-far`, `--cover-away`) only cost the win rate.

`hs_restaurant` (ph_restaurant_os, "PROP HUNT Side Hotel: Early Little Restaurant Concept", by Wolvindra Vinzuerio, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3270930065): the Workshop page states no licence and nothing forbids porting or sharing; credit, a link and removal on request is the arrangement, **this map is removed on request by the author**. Every texture and model is replaced by the game's own CC0 stand-ins. Imported with `--cover=40 --anchor=15 --slide-doors --terrain` (and `--clear` for the props the terrain buried): the author's first start is a stray in the basement office, so `--anchor` picks a ground-floor start on the east side as the seekers' lobby; the 96 hider starts are thinned to 60 and the 234 lights to 128, the most a document takes. It is in the pack but **banded in `headless_bots`** (the default maps): 24 rounds at medium, the hiders winning 8 and 17% of hiders never found, 18 of 18 checks. Getting there took three things that are not the importer's: the basement was reachable by no stair (hiders spawned in it, and three seekers spent a round pressed against the floor over it), so those hider starts were dropped and the bots now choose only places a seeker can walk to; the door frames' 7 cm posts and seven of the doors left a doorway too narrow for the bots' mesh, so those were removed or dropped (the doorways stay open); and `seeker_pace` 0.25 (the game's is 0.28) because a single seeker found every hider in a building this size otherwise. These edits were made to the document by hand (the import command was not kept); a re-import loses them.

`deco_mansion` (Gm_Deco_Mansion by Gastly_Furby_777, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3698815153): the Workshop page states no licence; credit, a link and removal on request is the arrangement, **this map is removed on request by the author**. Every texture and model is replaced by the game's own CC0 stand-ins and the map's own textures and sounds are not shipped. Imported with `--anchor=0` (the seekers start on the south lawn). The raw import has 13,312 boxes, far more than the wire allows, so the boxes were thinned by hand afterwards: every box under 0.5 m³ and shorter than 2.2 m in every dimension was dropped unless it is a step or carries a prop (4,429 remain), as was the sky ceiling slab, which only gave the walkable mesh a roof nobody can reach. The author's hider starts within 15 m of the seekers were dropped, 36 more were placed on floor the seekers can walk to (the lawn, the yard, and the lower and middle floors; the top floor was not joined to the rest on the walkable mesh, because the thinning had dropped half the steps of the spiral stair up to it, so 37 of those steps were put back from the unthinned import and 10 hiders start on the top floor), and the props, doors and spots the map check refused were removed. The document is not a clean re-import, and a re-import loses these edits. The converter has no option for either step yet.
`hs_hotel` (ph_hotel by 1Vodker1, https://gamebanana.com/mods/61495) is Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs 4.0; credit, a link and removal on request is the arrangement, **this map is removed on request by the author**. Every texture and model is replaced by the game's own CC0 stand-ins. Imported with `--scale=1.4 --cover=120 --cover-gap=1.5 --cover-clear=1.2 --lobby=14,2.5 --cover-away=16,8 --slide-doors --seekers-at=-12,-14 --cover-seed=3 --field=seeker_pace=0.25` plus `--drop-beside` for a bed and a side table that closed two doorways and `--drop-door` for all but one of the twelve doors (the one at 17.9, -22.8). It is in the pack and **banded in `headless_bots` on request** (`HS_BOTS_MAPS=hs_hotel`; not in the default rotation, which would move the other maps' statistics). What it took, after the earlier attempts (0 to 11 of 24 hiders won, doors stuck 10 to 66 s): the real-size hotel is about 40 by 38 m of 0.9 m doorways, so a seeker toured it in under a minute and the bots' mesh stuck at every door, and `--scale=1.4` (rooms, doorways and stairs grow, props do not) was the one change that moved both. Bot rounds, 24 at medium, three disjoint seed sets (`-- --first 0|24|48`) as originally imported (real size, 0.28 pace, no doors): hiders last 12.6 s, win 0 of 24, 0% never found; the current document: hiders last 169, 156 and 166 s, win 12, 10 and 9 of 24, 31, 24 and 18% never found, 0 hiders within 3 m of another, and 18, 17 and 18 of 18 checks (the 17 is a stand-in lingering 10.1 s beside the one door, band 9, on seeds 24 to 47). Every other door was dropped because each lingered 9 to 14 s somewhere in a set; `seeker_pace` 0.25 is as sensitive as it looks (0.28 gave hiders 0 of 24, 0.23 gave 15 to 19 of 24 and too few early finds). The importer's own `--no-doors` and `--widen-doors` stay for other maps.

`gm_villa` (Villa, by korka007, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2633639653): the Workshop page states no licence; credit, a link and removal on request is the arrangement, **this map is removed on request by the author**. Every texture and model is replaced by the game's own CC0 stand-ins, and none of the author's art is in the repository. **It is committed but not published to dot-cloud**: whether to publish a map with no stated licence is the owner's decision. Imported with `--terrain --no-doors --cover=40` (see `tools/import_bsp.py`) from a neutrally named copy of the `.bsp`, because the importer writes the file name into the blurb. The converter's output was 17426 boxes, over the 6000 a document takes, so it was trimmed by hand: boxes outside x -85..65 and z -40..87 were dropped, and so were those under 5 cm on any side or under 1 m on every side, which leaves the walls, floors and steps. The 1 spawn the source has became 28 hiders spread by farthest-point sampling over ground, terrace and house floors that a seeker's start reaches on foot (a flood fill over the box tops and the heightfield), and 4 seekers on the terrace beside the original start. The hedges of the garden are static props in the source, which the importer does not convert, so the hedge maze is not in the map. These edits were made to the document by hand (the post-processing was not kept); a re-import loses them.

## Drawn worlds (local only)

`tools/regen_worlds.sh` rebuilds every row of `tools/worlds.tsv` through dot-map's `bsp.py regen` into `world/hs_world_<id>/` (the stores in `world/.stores/`); the game links `world/` and a client draws the pack over the map's own boxes, which still collide (`HsMap.load_world`). `import_bsp.py` writes the document's `world` block (pack, scale, the offset that undoes its shift). Only the coverage json of each pack is tracked.

**These packs carry the source author's own faces and baked lighting, so they are derivatives, and nothing here may publish them. `tools/publish.sh` is the only publish command and excludes `world/` by name.** `hs_hotel`'s pack (ph_hotel, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) is built and tested locally only; publishing it, or any pack of `hs_apartment` (also NC-ND), waits for the owner's decision. `hs_frontyard` and `hs_restaurant` are drawn from packs regenerated with `--crop auto` (local only, the same decision applies); their offset is exact (low corners match to 0.15 m) and the pack stands up to 1.2 m off the boxes' top only where a roof is pitched or a wall brush runs on into the sky, which `headless_maps` allows for (`ROOF_SLACK`). Without the local `world/` a client draws the boxes, as before.

Ground that is a displacement in the source map is a `terrain` heightfield (dot-map's `dotbsp.terrain`, read by `DotMapTerrain`), not stepped boxes: `import_bsp.py` writes it, and `--graft-terrain=<map.json>` swaps it into a committed map while keeping every other box, prop and hand edit. `hs_restaurant` is the one map that has it.
